Report Overview

The global power tools market has entered a transformative phase in 2025, driven by rapid technological advancements, shifting consumer preferences toward cordless solutions, and unprecedented infrastructure investment worldwide. This report provides a data-driven, comprehensive analysis of the market landscape, growth trajectories, and strategic opportunities across all major regions and segments.

Valued at $38.5 billion in 2025, the market is projected to reach $52.8 billion by 2030, growing at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 6.5%. The cordless segment continues to dominate, accounting for 58% of total market revenue, as lithium-ion battery technology matures and brushless motor adoption accelerates across all product categories.

Key Highlights

  • Market Size: $38.5B (2025), projected $52.8B (2030) at 6.5% CAGR
  • Cordless Dominance: 58% market share, driven by battery innovation and user convenience
  • Asia-Pacific Leadership: Fastest-growing region at 8.2% CAGR, led by China and India
  • Brushless Adoption: 65% of new cordless models feature brushless motors
  • Professional Segment: 45% of revenue, highest-margin and most innovation-driven
  • Infrastructure Boom: $2.5T annual global infrastructure spending fueling demand

Report Contents

This 20+ page report covers the following topics in depth:

  • Market Size & Growth: Historical performance (2020-2024), 2025 valuation, and 2025-2030 CAGR forecasts with supporting data visualizations
  • Market Segmentation: Analysis by product type (drills, grinders, saws, wrenches, sanders), power source (cordless vs. corded), end user (professional, industrial, DIY), and price tier
  • Regional Analysis: Detailed examination of North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, Latin America, and Middle East & Africa, including market sizing, growth rates, and key country drivers
  • Growth Drivers: Five key growth factors including battery technology advancements, DIY culture expansion, IoT-enabled smart tools, and construction sector rebound
  • Competitive Landscape: Market share analysis of top 8 global brands, M&A activity, and the rise of Chinese manufacturers in the global supply chain
  • Trends & Challenges: Cordless revolution, sustainability/ESG imperatives, trade barrier impacts, and raw material price volatility
  • Investment Opportunities: Emerging market penetration, battery recycling ecosystem, Tool-as-a-Service models, and digital fleet management
