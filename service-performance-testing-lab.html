Performance Testing Lab

Professional testing equipment for motor power, torque, speed and battery performance evaluation.

Lab Overview

The Performance Testing Lab is HOLGENVY's primary facility dedicated to evaluating motor performance, power output characteristics, and battery system efficiency across our full range of professional power tools. This lab serves as the cornerstone of our quality assurance program, ensuring that every product meets or exceeds its published performance specifications before reaching the market. Equipped with advanced dynamometers, high-precision torque sensors, and state-of-the-art battery cyclers, the lab provides the quantitative data necessary to validate design parameters, verify manufacturing consistency, and support continuous product improvement initiatives.

Our Performance Testing Lab operates under strictly controlled environmental conditions to ensure measurement repeatability and data integrity. Temperature, humidity, and atmospheric pressure are continuously monitored and maintained within narrow tolerances, eliminating environmental variables that could affect test outcomes. All testing procedures follow documented protocols aligned with international standards, and every piece of equipment undergoes regular third-party calibration to maintain traceability and measurement accuracy. This commitment to precision enables HOLGENVY to deliver reliable, verifiable performance data that customers and regulatory bodies can trust.

Key Equipment

  • Dynamometer Test Bench — 0.1–50 Nm range with 0.1% measurement accuracy
  • Torque & Speed Measurement System — High-resolution optical encoder with real-time data capture
  • Battery Capacity Tester — 5V–100V range supporting multi-channel simultaneous testing
  • Power Analyzer — Multi-phase electrical measurement with harmonic analysis capability
  • Thermal Imaging Camera — Infrared thermography for heat distribution mapping under load
  • Data Acquisition System — High-speed multi-channel data logging with 100 kHz sampling rate

Testing Capabilities

  • Motor Power Curve Measurement — Full characterization of power output across the operational speed range
  • Torque-Speed Characterization — Mapping of torque delivery profiles under varying load conditions
  • Battery Discharge Profile Analysis — Evaluation of capacity, voltage curves, and discharge behavior under realistic load patterns
  • Runtime & Endurance Testing — Extended operation cycles to verify sustained performance and battery longevity claims
  • Thermal Performance Under Load — Monitoring of temperature rise and heat dissipation during continuous high-demand operation
  • Noise & Vibration Measurement — Acoustic and vibration profiling to assess mechanical integrity and user comfort

Standards Covered

HOLGENVY's Performance Testing Lab conducts all evaluations in compliance with internationally recognized standards, including IEC 60745 (hand-held motor-operated electric tools — safety), IEC 62841 (electric motor-operated tools — safety requirements for stationary and semi-stationary tools), UL 60745 (the United States equivalent standard for hand-held electric tools), and GB 3883 (the Chinese national standard for safety of hand-held electric tools). All laboratory equipment undergoes annual third-party calibration by accredited metrology laboratories, ensuring full traceability to national and international measurement standards. Calibration certificates and measurement uncertainty statements are maintained on file and available for audit upon request.

Testing Process

The performance testing workflow at HOLGENVY follows a structured, multi-stage process designed to ensure thoroughness and consistency. It begins with sample receiving and registration, where each test unit is assigned a unique tracking identifier and its specifications are verified against the product documentation. A visual inspection is then performed to confirm that the sample is free from shipping damage, manufacturing defects, or any condition that could compromise test validity. Following inspection, the test parameters are configured according to the applicable test protocol, including load profiles, measurement intervals, environmental conditions, and acceptance criteria.

During the performance testing phase, the sample is subjected to the prescribed test sequence while the data acquisition system continuously records all relevant parameters — including torque, speed, power, current, voltage, temperature, and vibration. Real-time monitoring allows test engineers to identify anomalies or unexpected behavior as they occur, enabling immediate investigation. Upon completion of the test sequence, the raw data is processed and analyzed against the predefined acceptance criteria and historical reference data from previous test runs.

The final stage of the process is report generation, where all findings are compiled into a comprehensive test report that includes test conditions, measurement data, statistical analysis, pass/fail determinations, and any observations or recommendations. Reports are reviewed by senior engineers before release, and all test data is archived in the laboratory's quality management system for traceability and future reference. This rigorous process ensures that every performance claim made by HOLGENVY is backed by verifiable, reproducible test data.
