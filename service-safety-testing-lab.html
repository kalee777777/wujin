Safety Testing Lab

Comprehensive safety testing including insulation resistance, grounding, and high-voltage endurance.

Lab Overview

The HOLGENVY Safety Testing Lab is a dedicated facility ensuring that every product meets rigorous international safety standards before it reaches the customer. Our lab operates as a critical checkpoint in the quality control pipeline, systematically verifying that all electrical, mechanical, and chemical safety requirements are satisfied across our entire product range. From handheld power tools to stationary equipment, no product leaves our facility without passing through this essential validation stage.

Our safety testing program covers three core domains: electrical safety, which includes insulation integrity, grounding reliability, and high-voltage endurance; mechanical safety, which evaluates structural strength, guard effectiveness, and hazard exposure; and chemical and material safety, which assesses material compliance, flame retardancy, and toxic substance restrictions. This comprehensive approach ensures that HOLGENVY products not only perform well but protect users under real-world operating conditions.

Standards Covered

HOLGENVY's Safety Testing Lab conducts all evaluations in strict compliance with internationally recognized safety standards, including IEC 62841-1 for electric motor-operated hand tools, UL 62841 for the North American market, EN 60745 for European compliance, and GB 4706 for Chinese market requirements. Our testing protocols are designed to support certification across all major regulatory frameworks, providing the documentation and test data needed for CE marking, UL listing, CSA certification, and CCC approval. This ensures that every HOLGENVY product can be confidently sold in global markets with full regulatory compliance.

Testing Process

The safety testing workflow at HOLGENVY follows a structured, multi-stage process designed to catch every potential hazard before products are shipped. It begins with an incoming inspection, where each unit is visually examined and its documentation verified against the product specification. This is followed by a comprehensive suite of electrical safety tests, including dielectric strength verification, insulation resistance measurement, ground continuity checks, and leakage current evaluation, all performed under both normal and abnormal operating conditions.

After electrical testing, products undergo mechanical safety checks to assess structural integrity, guard functionality, and resistance to impact and vibration. Selected units are then subjected to environmental exposure tests, simulating extreme temperature, humidity, and dust conditions to verify that safety performance holds under harsh real-world environments. Once all physical testing is complete, the certification documentation phase begins, where test results are compiled, reviewed, and formatted to support the relevant regulatory submissions.

The process concludes with the issuance of a final safety test report, which provides a detailed record of all tests performed, the standards applied, and the results obtained. This report serves as both an internal quality assurance record and an external certification support document, ensuring full traceability and transparency throughout the product lifecycle.
