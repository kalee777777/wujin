Canton Fair 2025 — Phase 1

Our team showcased the latest brushless drill series and OEM solutions to buyers from 40+ countries.

Event Overview

The China Import and Export Fair, widely known as the Canton Fair, is the largest and most comprehensive trade fair in China. Established in 1957, it has become a vital platform for international trade, drawing buyers and exhibitors from every corner of the globe. The fair is held biannually in Guangzhou and is divided into three phases, each focusing on different product categories.

HOLGENVY participated in Phase 1 of the 137th Canton Fair, which took place from April 15 to 19, 2025. Phase 1 focuses on electronics, electrical products, and hardware — a perfect match for our power tool portfolio. With over 20,000 exhibitors spread across the sprawling Pazhou Complex, the event offered an unparalleled opportunity to connect with global buyers, showcase our latest innovations, and strengthen existing partnerships. Our booth was located in the Tools & Hardware section, where we welcomed visitors from morning to closing each day.

Highlights

  • Launched 3 new brushless drill models with enhanced motor efficiency and ergonomic design
  • Collected 200+ business cards from international buyers and distributors
  • Generated 25+ qualified leads with confirmed follow-up meetings scheduled
  • Met with buyers from Southeast Asia, Europe, the Middle East, and South America
  • Received strong interest in OEM and ODM partnership opportunities

Products Showcased

  • Brushless Impact Drill — 20V platform with 3-speed gearbox and LED work light
  • Cordless Angle Grinder — compact design with overload protection and soft start
  • High-Torque Impact Wrench — 700 Nm max torque for heavy-duty fastening applications
  • Electric Tool Kit — 6-piece combo kit targeting professional contractors and installers
  • OEM Customization Samples — branded housings, custom color schemes, and private-label packaging

Client Feedback

Throughout the five-day event, we engaged in detailed conversations with buyers from diverse markets. A recurring theme was the strong demand for customization capabilities — many distributors expressed interest in private-label programs tailored to their regional markets. Buyers were particularly impressed by our in-house R&D capacity and the flexibility of our OEM services, which allow for custom motor specifications, housing designs, and packaging configurations.

Quality certifications also drew significant attention. Our ISO 9001 and CE compliance gave buyers confidence in product consistency and regulatory readiness for their home markets. Several European clients specifically noted the importance of these certifications for market entry, while Southeast Asian buyers were keen to discuss delivery lead times and MOQ flexibility for faster inventory turnover.

Looking Ahead

Following the success of Phase 1, HOLGENVY is already preparing for Canton Fair Phase 2, where we plan to expand our display area and introduce additional cordless tool platforms. We are also evaluating participation in several upcoming international exhibitions, including hardware shows in the Middle East and Southeast Asia later this year. Our goal remains to build lasting partnerships with distributors worldwide and continue delivering innovative, reliable power tool solutions. We look forward to seeing both new and familiar faces at our next event.
