Factory Overview

World-Class Facility

Located in No. 9, Nanliu Road, Chengyang District, Qingdao City, Shandong Province — one of China's most important manufacturing hubs — our factory spans over 10,000 square meters of modern production space. Equipped with state-of-the-art machinery and automated assembly lines, we deliver consistent quality at scale.

Our facility includes dedicated zones for raw material storage, component manufacturing, product assembly, quality testing, and finished goods warehousing. Every zone is designed for efficiency, safety, and traceability.

With a monthly production capacity of 50,000+ units and flexible MOQ support, we can handle orders of any size — from small trial batches to large-scale container shipments.
